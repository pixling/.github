# Code of Conduct

Help keep Pixling's issues and discussions welcoming and useful for everyone.

- Be respectful, including across differences in language, experience, and identity.
- Discuss ideas and behavior constructively; do not attack people.
- No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, spam, or impersonation.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's conversations, files, account names, or personal information without permission.

This applies to Pixling's GitHub community spaces. Maintainers may moderate content or limit participation to protect the community, considering the context and severity of the behavior.

Report concerns privately to [support@pixling.app](mailto:support@pixling.app). Share only the details needed to understand the concern. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis, with replies in English.
