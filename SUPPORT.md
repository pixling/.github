# Support

Support is **best-effort**. We reply in **English**; Japanese submissions are also welcome, and reports may be submitted in any language. We cannot promise a response time or a fix.

- Documentation: https://pixling.app/docs
- Bugs: https://github.com/pixling/pixling/issues/new/choose
- Ideas and questions: https://github.com/pixling/pixling/discussions (Ideas / Q&A)
- Billing, refunds, transaction IDs, wallet details, or personal circumstances: [support@pixling.app](mailto:support@pixling.app)
- Security: https://github.com/pixling/pixling/security/advisories/new (private). If unavailable, email support.

Screenshots and logs can expose resident or agent conversations, file names, and your Mac account name. Review and redact before posting. Never post billing, refund, or transaction details in public issues or discussions.

We do not use a stale bot or automatically close reports merely for inactivity. This intake repository does not accept pull requests; please use issues and discussions.
